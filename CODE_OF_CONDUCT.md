# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Web Agent Gateway welcomes technical collaboration that is respectful, specific, and focused on improving the project.

Participants are expected to:
- discuss people and ideas professionally;
- assume good faith while still challenging technical claims with evidence;
- respect privacy, security boundaries, and user-controlled authority;
- keep reviews focused on the change, its risks, and its verification;
- avoid spam, harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or deliberate disruption.

## Security-sensitive conduct

Do not publish credentials, private repository material, sensitive local-machine data, or exploit details that could put users at risk. Follow `SECURITY.md` for vulnerability reporting.

Do not use community channels to manufacture stars, downloads, endorsements, or other popularity signals.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close or lock disruptive threads, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect contributors or the project.

When possible, concerns should be raised through GitHub's reporting tools. If a private maintainer contact is needed and no private channel is available, open a minimal public issue requesting one without including sensitive details.

This code applies to the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project-managed collaboration spaces.
